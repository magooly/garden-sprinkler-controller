    Full system backup and restore

    Encrypted backup of all saved channels, timetables, Rain Pause, relay connections, Tapo assignments and credentials, the standard phone password, activity history and service configuration. Not an Ubuntu image. Active runs are never restored.

    Choose a separate backup password of at least 12 characters. Store it in a password manager or a safe place separate from the backup. It is not your Tapo or phone password. Lost backup passwords cannot be recovered.

    Timed runs stop automatically while the controller is working. RUN UNTIL STOPPED requires a saved master pump exclusion and no manual maximum; it has no time limit, even if your phone disconnects. Adding or lowering a manual maximum stops an active manual run. Rain Pause exemptions still apply. Manual runs stop on controller restart; communication failure can prevent an OFF command from reaching the relay. The master pump is automatic.
